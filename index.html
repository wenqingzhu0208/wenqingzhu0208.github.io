About Me

Hi, I'm a Ph.D. candidate at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC), working in the Golding Lab. With a background in chemistry and physics, I study how bacteriophages infect their bacterial hosts, one virus and one genome at a time.

My research combines super-resolution fluorescence microscopy, click-chemistry labeling, and computational modeling. Using 3D MINFLUX nanoscopy with EdU-labeled phage DNA, I measure the nanoscale shape and organization of single phage genomes after they enter E. coli, and use polymer physics to interpret their structure. I also study phage motility and DNA injection with optical trapping, in collaboration with the Chemla Lab.
